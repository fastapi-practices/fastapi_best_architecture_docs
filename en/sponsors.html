Placement notes

How is pricing set?

Booths are priced per month after a short conversation. Mention the tier and 1 / 3 / 12 month duration in your email or DM, and we will reply with a quote and schedule

Can you issue invoices?

Booth sponsorship is currently voluntary support, so we cannot issue invoices yet

Do you guarantee clicks or conversions?

We cannot guarantee specific conversion numbers. We do guarantee the agreed placement and duration

What products are a fit?

We prefer developer-related products such as tools, cloud, courses, and hardware. Unrelated categories can be reviewed case by case. Illegal or gray-area products are declined
