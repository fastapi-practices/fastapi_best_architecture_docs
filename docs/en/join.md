The growth of [FastAPI Practices](https://github.com/fastapi-practices) and its ecosystem depends on the open-source community. If you enjoy our work and would like to take part, we would be honored.

We evaluate based on your PRs and engagement, and warmly invite you to join our [team](https://github.com/orgs/fastapi-practices/people), or add your profile to our [website](./team.md). If you are interested, contact us on Discord and provide the following information (you can copy and fill this in):

```
**Nickname:** xxx
**Bio:** xxx
**GitHub:** xxx
**City:** City + Province/State
**Homepage:** xxx (defaults to GitHub)
**PR link:** xxx (if any)
```
